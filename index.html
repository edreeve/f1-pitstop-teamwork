01 · The Problem They Discovered
The riskiest moment wasn't surgery — it was the handover.
At Great Ormond Street Hospital (GOSH), moving critically ill children from the operating theatre to ICU meant brilliant specialists all stepping in at once—leading to overlapping tasks, crowded spaces, and missed clinical details.
02 · The Unlikely Partnership
They didn't hire better doctors — they called Formula 1.
GOSH brought in Ferrari & McLaren to redesign surgical transfers using pit-stop choreography—followed by Williams F1 partnering with University Hospital of Wales (Cardiff) to transform emergency newborn resuscitation.
What happened when NHS hospitals applied Formula 1 choreography? Let's look at the peer-reviewed results →
